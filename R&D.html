Testing tips
  • MacBook Air M4: turn Center Stage off (menu bar → green camera icon / Control Center → Video Effects) — its auto-zoom changes face size and head pose and spoils calibration.
  • Calibrate looking where you would look at the road. Enable Two-step calibration to also measure your closed-eye level (most accurate).
  • Use the Record frames button, reproduce a problem, and download the CSV to see exactly what each signal did.
  • Compare features one at a time: watch the chart and the event log while toggling.
  • Android Chrome: the camera only works on HTTPS or localhost. Host the file on GitHub Pages/Netlify, or use adb reverse tcp:8000 tcp:8000 and open http://localhost:8000 on the phone.
  • Offline: put vision_bundle.mjs, the wasm folder and face_landmarker.task in a mediapipe/ folder next to this file and open it with ?local.
